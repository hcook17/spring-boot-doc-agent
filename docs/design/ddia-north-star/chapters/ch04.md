# Chapter 4. Encoding Formats and Modes of Dataflow (bridge into ch05)

## One-sentence thesis

How data moves (DB, RPC, async messages) constrains encoding and evolution options.

## Who this chapter is for

API and pipeline authors.

## What it covers

Encoding formats and dataflow modes at a survey level (deepen via ch05).

## When to apply

## Where it shows up in systems

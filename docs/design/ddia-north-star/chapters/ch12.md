# Chapter 12. Stream Processing

## One-sentence thesis

Streams derive continuous views with fresher latency — still derived, still need SoR discipline.

## Who this chapter is for

Authors of incremental/watch-mode pipelines.

## What it covers

Continuous derivation vs batch.

## When to apply

Choosing streaming freshness over batch cost.

## Where it shows up in systems

Future incremental doc pipelines; target Kafka/stream apps.

## Why it matters

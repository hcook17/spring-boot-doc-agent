# Chapter 7. Partitioning

## One-sentence thesis

Partitioning scales by splitting data — skew and cross-partition operations become first-class costs.

## Who this chapter is for

Engineers sharding work (e.g. Stage-4 fan-out, group partitions).

## What it covers

How work and data are split across nodes/groups.

## When to apply

Capacity and fan-out designs (adoption L2).

## Where it shows up in systems

partition_repo, capacity_preflight, group edges.

## Why it matters

Wrong partition key creates permanent hotspots and band-aid caches.

## How to use it here

Name partition key and skew plan before coding; avoid silent rebalance assumptions.

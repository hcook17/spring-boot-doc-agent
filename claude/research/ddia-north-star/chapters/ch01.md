# Chapter 1. Trade-Offs in Data Systems Architecture

## One-sentence thesis

Architecture is tradeoffs among operational vs analytical needs, SoR vs derived data, and distribution costs.

## Section map

- Operational Versus Analytical Systems
- Systems of Record and Derived Data
- Cloud Versus Self-Hosting
- Distributed Versus Single-Node Systems
- Data Systems, Law, and Society

## Digested claims

- Operational vs analytical workloads differ in access patterns.
- SoR holds each fact once; derived data is recomputable.
- On discrepancy, SoR wins by definition.
- Cloud and distribution are tradeoffs, not free upgrades.
